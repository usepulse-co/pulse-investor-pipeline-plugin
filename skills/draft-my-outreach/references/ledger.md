# The outreach ledger

A running record of where the founder is. You hold it in the conversation and print it whenever they ask or when a session ends. Export it for the founder to retain; new sessions require them to supply it again.

## Shape

```
Outreach ledger, 5 Oct 2026

Wave 1 (likely leads, good fit)
  Firm A    email, pitch address     sent 5 Oct
  Firm B    application form         sent 5 Oct
  Firm C    intro blurb              drafted (intro ask not yet sent)
  Firm D    email, general inbox     skipped (founder: wait for the GC signing)

Wave 2 (top targets, after wave 1 replies)
  Firm E, Firm F, Firm G

Wave 3 (followers and stretch, once a lead is in)
  Firm H, Firm I, Firm J, Firm K

Founder-agreed reminders: Firm A on 12 Oct if no reply. Firm B: check 19 Oct.
```

## Statuses

`to draft`, `drafted`, `sent`, `skipped`, `replied`, `meeting`, `passed`, `no reply`. The founder moves a firm by saying so ("sent", "they replied", "skip D"). Record the founder's stated event date, or label it unknown and separately note when reported. Drafts and page views never imply sent, replied or meeting. A meeting requires the founder to report one was arranged.

The route column names the kind of address the founder used, never the address or a person. "email, pitch address" or "email, general inbox" is enough.

## Follow-up dates

Use the founder's chosen dates and the firm's published guidance. If no cadence is given, propose one as a preference for approval. Forms stay on the form route: a published email alone does not authorize switching channels. Draft intro nudges only when asked.

Show "follow-ups due" only for agreed dates and confirmed sent events. Otherwise label dates unplanned.

## Waves

The ledger carries the wave plan from step 2 of the skill, so the founder can see at a glance that followers are waiting on a lead. When the founder reports a lead replied with interest, mark replied and offer to open the next wave; mark meeting only after an arranged meeting is reported.

## Pulse

No available tool stores or retrieves this outreach ledger in Pulse. Print or export the table. Deck engagement may be shown alongside founder-reported sends in the current table, but does not update outreach status.
